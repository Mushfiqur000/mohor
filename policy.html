Pay the rider in cash when your parcel arrives. Please check the parcel in front of the rider.

Order confirmation

Once you place an order on the website or via WhatsApp, our team verifies availability and calls or messages you with the final bill, including delivery charge, before dispatch.

24-hour exchange policy

We take pride in the quality of our handcrafted clothing. If you receive a defective, wrong-size or incorrect item, please notify us within 24 hours of receiving the delivery.

Return conditions

  • The item must be unused, unwashed and in its original packaging with tags intact.
  • Please record an unboxing video to claim any damage or defect.
  • Exchanges are subject to stock; if unavailable we offer another piece of equal value.
  • Delivery charges for exchanges caused by our mistake are covered by us.

Colour disclaimer

Actual colours may vary slightly due to lighting during photography or your device's display. Exchanges are not accommodated purely for slight colour variations.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to deliver your order: your name, phone number, address and, if you create an account, your email.

We never sell your information. It is shared only with our delivery partner to bring your parcel to you. Passwords are stored securely hashed.

You can edit your profile or permanently delete your account at any time from your account page.
