About Mohor Clothings

Welcome to Mohor Clothings, your premier destination for handcrafted luxury fashion in Bangladesh. From our breathable, premium soft cotton Three-Piece ensembles to our elegantly tailored Kurtis and authentic Khadi wear, every piece is designed with the modern woman in mind. Whether you are stepping into a university classroom, leading a corporate meeting, or celebrating a festive occasion, our collections offer the perfect fit. Proudly serving Sylhet and customers nationwide, we are dedicated to bringing you high-quality embroidery and timeless designs that empower your everyday wardrobe.
